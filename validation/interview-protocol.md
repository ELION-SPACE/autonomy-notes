# Mission-Operations Problem-Discovery Protocol

**Status:** Pre-interview research protocol  
**Scope:** Small-spacecraft mission planning and operations  
**Current evidence:** See the dated [canonical discovery log](https://github.com/ELION-SPACE/ELION-SPACE/blob/main/DISCOVERY_LOG.md); outreach is not validated customer evidence.

## Purpose

This protocol is designed to determine whether small-spacecraft mission teams experience a recurring and consequential operational problem that could justify a narrowly scoped software demonstration.

It establishes the questions, evidence requirements and decision rules before interviews begin. This prevents ELION-SPACE from treating polite interest, isolated opinions or founder assumptions as market validation.

## Research question

Do small-spacecraft mission teams experience a recurring and consequential coordination problem involving payload activities, pointing requirements, ground-station opportunities or spacecraft constraints?

## Interview introduction

This is problem-discovery research, not a sales conversation. I am not seeking confidential, restricted or mission-sensitive information. I want to understand actual operational workflows before deciding whether further software development is justified.

## Questions

1. Please describe the most recent mission-planning or replanning situation that required substantial manual coordination.
2. What triggered the work, and which people, systems and constraints were involved?
3. Which part consumed the most time or created the greatest risk of error?
4. How frequently does this situation occur, and what happens if it is handled poorly?
5. Which tools or workarounds are currently used?
6. What prevents the existing workflow from being improved?
7. What narrowly scoped demonstration would be credible enough for your team to review?
8. Who else has direct experience with this problem and might be willing to speak with me?

## Evidence to capture

For every interview, record:

- Respondent's role and relevant operational experience
- A concrete past example
- Frequency of the problem
- Time or personnel burden
- Operational consequence
- Existing tools and workarounds
- Whether the problem is already adequately solved
- Requirements for a credible demonstration
- Any referral offered
- Direct quotations only when explicit permission is given

## Evidence that does not count

The following must not be treated as validation:

- General encouragement
- Compliments about the idea
- Expressions of interest without a concrete operational example
- Problems mentioned only after leading prompts
- Founder assumptions
- Unfinished prototypes
- Unverified estimates of cost or workload
- A single interview

## Decision rule

Do not build a product from one conversation.

The [canonical build gate](https://github.com/ELION-SPACE/ELION-SPACE/blob/main/DISCOVERY_LOG.md#build-gate)
applies: five qualified interviews, at least three actual users/operators, the
same recurring problem independently described in three interviews, evidence of
burden/consequence, and a qualified reviewer for a precisely scoped demonstration.
These conditions are cumulative. Inconclusive or contradictory evidence calls for
more discovery or rejection of the hypothesis, not a product build.

## Confidentiality

Do not publish names, organisations, contact details, mission details or quotations without permission. Public interview notes must be anonymised.

## Intended decision

The completed interviews must support one of three decisions:

1. Build one narrowly scoped demonstration.
2. Conduct additional interviews because the evidence remains inconclusive.
3. Reject or replace the current problem hypothesis.
